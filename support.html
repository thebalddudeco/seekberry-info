How to play

Drag across a complete word in a straight line, or tap its first and last letters. Words can run horizontally, vertically, or diagonally, in either direction. Found words are highlighted and checked off. Hints reveal the first letter of a remaining word.

Local progress

Progress is saved on this device. Open About Seekberry to clear it. No account is needed. The mobile app bundles all current puzzles and fonts for offline play.
