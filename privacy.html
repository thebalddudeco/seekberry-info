Privacy policy

Who publishes Seekberry

Seekberry is published by Justin Brown. Effective date: October 22, 2026. This policy describes version 0.1.0 of the packaged Android, iPhone, and iPad app.

Puzzle progress

Seekberry saves your selected puzzle, found words, and puzzle completion locally on your device. On Android and Apple devices, this uses the operating system’s app preferences. There is no Seekberry account or progress-sync server. Operating system backup settings may include app data in backups managed by your device provider.

Data collection and sharing

This version does not send puzzle progress or other app-usage data to the publisher. It includes no advertising, tracking, analytics, third-party crash-reporting SDK, or generative AI service. It does not request access to your location, contacts, photos, camera, or microphone. Puzzles, artwork, and fonts are bundled with the app.

Purchases and platform services

Seekberry is a paid download with no advertisements or in-app purchases in this version. Apple or Google handles your store purchase, billing, refunds, and any platform diagnostics under its own terms and privacy policy. Seekberry does not receive your payment-card details. If you contact us by email, your email provider and ours process that correspondence.

Support emails

If you email theonetheonlyvonbraun@gmail.com, we receive the address and information you choose to send. We use that information to respond to your request, resolve support issues, and meet any applicable legal obligations. Please avoid sending sensitive information that is not needed for support. We do not use support emails for advertising.

Your choices and deletion

Use About Seekberry → Clear saved puzzle progress to remove the progress stored by the app. You can also manage or remove app data using your device’s settings. Backups, store transaction records, and email correspondence are managed separately; deleting local progress does not erase those records. To request deletion of support correspondence held by the publisher, use the contact address below.

Audience

Seekberry is intended for a general audience and is not specifically directed to children. This build does not ask anyone to submit personal information. If you believe a child has sent personal information through support, contact us so we can review and remove it where appropriate.

Website and browser preview

The public Seekberry information pages are hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub processes network information such as IP addresses and request logs under its own privacy statement. These pages contain no publisher-operated analytics or contact forms. If you use a browser preview of the game, your browser saves progress locally and may cache the app for offline use. The packaged mobile app does not need to load this website for gameplay.

Changes and contact

We will update this policy when the app’s data practices change. Privacy inquiries and requests: theonetheonlyvonbraun@gmail.com.
